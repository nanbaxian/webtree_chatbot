## 3. About Webtree Academy

### Core description
Webtree Academy describes itself as an educational academy in Toronto using an innovative, creative, and nurturing approach to provide personalized, high-quality education.

### Academic support and services described
- Diversified programs supporting different academic needs
- Customized academic consulting
- Career planning
- Credit courses
- Accelerated Pathways Program
- Tutoring support
- Comprehensive university application guidance
- Year-round operation including summer session

### Faculty profile described
The school says many teachers hold Master’s or Doctoral degrees and/or are certified by the Ontario College of Teachers (OCT).

### “Our Unique Story” / selling points captured
- Founded to provide exceptional, personalized education
- Small class sizes
- Tailored support programs
- Focus on both academics and mentorship
- Claimed class size cap of 10 students
- Claimed 100% of graduates accepted into universities or colleges of first choice

### Key highlights listed on the About page
- 6:1 average student-to-teacher ratio
- 100% university placement rate
- Customized educational plans
- Accelerated Pathway Program
- Enriched extracurricular activities
- 8+ specialized STEM workshops annually
- Academic competitions and contests
- Specialized faculty with advanced credentials
- Technology integration across learning

### Mission
Webtree states its mission is to equip students with linguistic, academic, and personal skills needed to achieve educational and life goals, while inspiring curiosity, fostering creativity, and cultivating resilience.

### Diversity, Equity and Inclusion
The About page includes sections on:

These sections describe the school’s stated approach to inclusive and respectful treatment of all people, opportunity creation, and welcoming participation.

## 7. Fees and Tuition

## 2026–2027 Tuition

### Domestic
- Grades 7 through Grade 12: **$23,000**

### International
- Grades 7 through Grade 12: **$27,000**

### Elite Program – Academic Enrichment
- Grades 7 through Grade 11: **$8,800**
- Grade 12: **$10,800**

### Program and Supplemental Fees
- Application/Registration Fee: **$300**
- Technology, Material & Incidental Fee: **$1,000**
- School Co-curricular Fee: **$2,500**
- Medical Insurance (International Students only): **$730**

### Notes shown on page
- Domestic students are defined as Canadian citizens or permanent residents whose first language is English or French.
- Proof of local residence with a parent, grandparent or aunt/uncle must be provided.
- Additional costs include uniform, books, and overnight school trips.
