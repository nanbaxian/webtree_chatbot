# Canada and US Competitions and Major Exams

This guide covers the most common tests and competitions families ask about in Canada and the US. Dates change every year, so use the official pages for current registration windows and administration details.

## Quick Map

- `SSAT`: private school admission test for grades 3-11.
- `SAT`: digital college admissions test with Reading and Writing plus Math.
- `ACT`: college readiness test with English, Math, Reading, and optional Science and Writing.
- `AP`: advanced placement exams for subject credit, placement, and enrichment.
- `TOEFL iBT` and `IELTS Academic`: English proficiency tests for school and university applications.
- `AMC`, `AIME`, `Euclid`: math contest pathway for enrichment and olympiad-style problem solving.
- `CCC`: Canadian Computing Competition for programming and algorithms.

## Admissions and Standardized Tests

### SSAT

- The SSAT is a private school admission test.
- It is used for students in grades 3-11.
- The test includes verbal, math, and reading sections, plus a non-scored writing sample.
- Paper SSAT is usually offered on established dates between October and March.
- Families often ask about SSAT when applying to independent schools in Canada or the US.

### SAT

- The SAT is a digital college admissions test.
- It has two sections: Reading and Writing, and Math.
- The current digital SAT takes 2 hours and 14 minutes total.
- The SAT is widely used for US college admissions and is also recognized by many Canadian universities.

### ACT

- The ACT is a college readiness test.
- It includes English, Math, and Reading, with optional Science and Writing.
- Many US colleges accept either SAT or ACT.
- Students usually choose based on strengths, timing, and school preference.

### AP Exams

- AP stands for Advanced Placement.
- AP exams are usually administered once a year in May.
- Many AP exams now use Bluebook in fully digital or hybrid digital format.
- AP scores are commonly used for college credit, placement, or admissions context.

### TOEFL iBT

- TOEFL iBT measures academic English.
- It tests listening, reading, speaking, and writing.
- It is commonly used for university admission and English proficiency proof.

### IELTS Academic

- IELTS Academic tests listening, reading, writing, and speaking.
- It is widely accepted by universities and other institutions.
- Families often compare IELTS with TOEFL when they need an English proficiency test.

## Math Competitions

### AMC 8, AMC 10, AMC 12

- The American Mathematics Competitions are the best-known K-12 math contests in North America.
- AMC 8 is aimed at middle school and early secondary students.
- AMC 10 is for grade 10 and below.
- AMC 12 is for grade 12 and below.
- AMC 10 and AMC 12 are 25-question, 75-minute multiple-choice contests.
- AMC results are often used as a first step toward higher-level math contests.

### AIME

- AIME is the American Invitational Mathematics Examination.
- It is an invite-only contest for students who perform well on AMC 10 or AMC 12.
- It is a 15-question, 3-hour exam with integer answers from 0 to 999.
- Strong AIME performance can lead to USAMO or USAJMO invitations.

### Euclid

- The Euclid Contest is a senior-level math contest run by the University of Waterloo CEMC.
- It is written annually and is widely known in Canada and internationally.
- It is aimed at senior secondary students and uses proof-style problem solving.

## Computing Competitions

### CCC

- The Canadian Computing Competition is an online programming contest run by Waterloo CEMC.
- It is aimed at secondary students with a range of programming experience.
- CCC has Junior and Senior levels.
- Strong CCC performance can help with computer science pathway planning and, in some cases, admissions context.

## How Families Usually Choose

- If the goal is private school admission, start with SSAT.
- If the goal is US college admission, compare SAT and ACT.
- If the goal is college credit or advanced coursework, look at AP.
- If the goal is English proficiency for university admission, look at TOEFL iBT or IELTS Academic.
- If the goal is math enrichment or olympiad pathways, start with AMC and then AIME or Euclid.
- If the goal is programming enrichment, start with CCC.

## By Grade

- Grade 3-4: SSAT Elementary is the relevant private school test family.
- Grade 5-7: SSAT Middle Level is the usual private school test family.
- Grade 8-10: AMC 8 and sometimes SSAT Middle or Upper, depending on the goal.
- Grade 9-10: AMC 10, CCC Junior or Senior, SAT prep, ACT prep, and AP planning often start here.
- Grade 11: SAT, ACT, AP, AMC 10/12, CCC Senior, and Euclid become more relevant.
- Grade 12: SAT, ACT, AP, TOEFL/IELTS, AMC 12, CCC Senior, and Euclid are common.

## Prep Path

- For SSAT, focus on vocabulary, reading speed, quantitative reasoning, and timed practice.
- For SAT, focus on reading and writing patterns, algebra, advanced math, and Bluebook familiarity.
- For ACT, focus on pace, grammar, math fluency, reading speed, and optional science if needed.
- For AP, focus on course content, free-response practice, and the specific Bluebook or hybrid exam mode.
- For AMC, focus on algebra, counting, number theory, geometry, and contest strategy.
- For AIME, focus on proof-style thinking, multi-step problem solving, and deeper contest math.
- For CCC, focus on Python, Java, or C++, algorithm practice, and debugging under time pressure.
- For TOEFL and IELTS, focus on academic listening, reading, speaking fluency, and writing structure.
