# Ontario University Program Seed

This seed set captures a small group of high-frequency Ontario undergraduate programs and their official admission requirements from current university pages.

It is intentionally focused on questions students ask most often:
- what courses are required
- what average is competitive
- whether a supplemental application is required
- whether there are recommended or preferred courses

Tuition, scholarships, and full curriculum maps can be added in a second pass.

## University of Waterloo

### Computer Science
- Campus: Waterloo
- Ontario students need six Grade 12 U and/or M courses including Advanced Functions, Calculus and Vectors, any Grade 12 U English, and one other 4U course.
- Recommended: Grade 11 U Introduction to Computer Science.
- Admission average: individual selection from the low to mid-90s.
- Application note: apply directly; the Admission Information Form is required.

### Software Engineering
- Ontario students need six Grade 12 U and/or M courses including Advanced Functions, Calculus and Vectors, Chemistry, Physics, and English.
- The page also says applicants need programming experience, such as Grade 11 or 12 computing/information science or equivalent, or self-study with a demonstrated interest in solving open problems, debugging code, testing code, and learning new software languages.
- Admission average: individual selection from the low to mid-90s.
- Application note: the Admission Information Form is required and online interviews are required.

### Computing and Financial Management
- Ontario students need six Grade 12 U and/or M courses including any Grade 12 U English with a minimum final grade of 75%, Advanced Functions, Calculus and Vectors, and one other Grade 12 U course.
- Recommended: Introduction to Computer Science and Principles of Financial Accounting.
- Admission average: individual selection from the low to mid-90s.
- Application note: the Admission Information Form is required.
- Extra note: Waterloo strongly encourages the Canadian Senior Mathematics Contest and/or Euclid Mathematics Contest.

### Waterloo tuition snapshot
- Waterloo says first-year tuition varies by program and student type.
- Example first-year Ontario domestic fees shown on the page: Computer Science $16,000, Software Engineering $21,000, Computing and Financial Management $11,000.
- Waterloo co-op students pay a fee of $836 per school term.

### Waterloo math entrance scholarships
- The Faculty of Mathematics awards 200 entrance scholarships.
- Values shown include $10,000 over four years, $8,000 over four years, and one-year scholarships ranging from $1,000 to $5,000.
- Students must write the Euclid Mathematics Contest or the Canadian Senior Mathematics Contest and submit an Admission Information Form to be considered.

### Data Science
- Ontario students need six Grade 12 U and/or M courses including Advanced Functions, Calculus and Vectors, any Grade 12 U English, and one other 4U course.
- Recommended: Grade 11 U Introduction to Computer Science.
- Admission average: individual selection from the mid-80s if applying through Mathematics and low to mid-90s if applying through Computer Science.
- Application note: apply to Computer Science or Mathematics and choose Data Science as your major at the end of first year; the Admission Information Form is required.

### Accounting and Financial Management
- Ontario students need Any Grade 12 U English with a minimum grade of 75, Advanced Functions with a minimum grade of 75, and Calculus and Vectors with a minimum grade of 75.
- Admission average: mid-80s.
- Recommended background: Grade 12 Principles of Financial Accounting and Grade 12 Mathematics of Data Management.

## University of Toronto

### Computer Science at U of T Mississauga
- OSSD with six subjects at the 4U/M level, including English.
- The co-op component of Grade 12 courses is not used for admission purposes.
- Ontario secondary school qualifications shown on the page: ENG4U / EAE4U, MHF4U, and MCV4U.

### Computer Science at U of T Scarborough
- OSSD with six subjects at the 4U/M level, including English.
- The co-op component of Grade 12 courses is not used for admission purposes.
- Ontario secondary school qualifications shown on the page: ENG4U / EAE4U, MCV4U, and MHF4U.

### Commerce
- OSSD with six subjects at the 4U/M level, including English.
- The co-op component of Grade 12 courses is not used for admission purposes.
- Ontario secondary school qualifications shown on the page: ENG4U / EAE4U, MCV4U, and MHF4U.

### Life Sciences
- OSSD with six subjects at the 4U/M level, including English.
- The U of T viewbook shows no additional requirements beyond English for Life Sciences.
- This is a broad life sciences entry path that leads to multiple programs under the HBSc umbrella.

### Engineering Science
- OSSD with six subjects at the 4U/M level, including English.
- The co-op component of Grade 12 courses is not used for admission purposes.
- Ontario secondary school qualifications shown on the page: ENG4U / EAE4U, MHF4U, MCV4U, SCH4U, and SPH4U.
- Supplemental application required.

### U of T awards snapshot
- U of T says most entrance scholarships and admission awards are automatic and do not require a separate application.
- The Awards Profile is used for awards based on financial need, leadership, community involvement, and other non-academic criteria.
- The President's Scholars of Excellence Program is about 150 awards at $15,000 in the first year.
- The University of Toronto Scholars Program is about 900 awards at $10,000.

## Toronto Metropolitan University

### Business Management
- Ontario high school applicants need the OSSD, six Grade 12 U or M courses, and a minimum overall average of 70%.
- Required Grade 12 U courses: English and Mathematics, with ENG4U/EAE4U preferred and one of MCV4U, MHF4U, or MDM4U accepted.
- MCV4U is preferred.
- Due to competition, the average needed can fluctuate year to year.

### Computer Science
- Ontario high school applicants need the OSSD, six Grade 12 U or M courses, and a minimum overall average of 70%.
- Required Grade 12 U courses: ENG4U/EAE4U preferred, MHF4U, one of MCV4U or MDM4U, and one of SPH4U, SCH4U, or SBI4U.
- Due to competition, the average needed can fluctuate year to year.

### Accounting & Finance
- This is a grades-only program.
- Ontario high school applicants need the OSSD, six Grade 12 U or M courses, and a minimum overall average of 70%.
- Required Grade 12 U courses: ENG4U/EAE4U preferred, MHF4U, and MCV4U.
- The page shows a grade range in the mid-80s.

### TMU tuition snapshot
- TMU says full-time undergraduate Ontario students pay about $7,284 to $11,738 for the 2025-2026 academic year, depending on program and residency.
- TMU's Accounting & Finance current-students fee schedule shows Year 1 at $12,525.94 for domestic out-of-province rates.

### TMU scholarship snapshot
- TMU says it has over $7 million designated for entrance scholarship support.
- For students admitted for 2026-2027 with a Canadian secondary school average of 86% or higher, TMU guarantees a renewable entrance scholarship.
- The renewable award values shown are $3,000 at 95%+, $1,500 at 90-94.99%, and $750 at 86-89.99%.
- TMU's President's Entrance Scholarship is $40,000 total, paid as $10,000 per year and renewable.
- TMU's International Student Leadership Award is $10,000 for up to 53 recipients.

## McMaster University

### Honours Health Sciences
- The program page says the Honours Health Sciences program requires a course that is not math, science, or technology-based for admission to Level I.
- The page also shows an anticipated admission average in the mid-80s.
- The supplementary applications page lists Honours Health Sciences as having a supplementary application due in the early/mid-February window.

### iBioMed
- McMaster describes iBioMed as a blended engineering and health sciences program with limited enrolment of 155 students per year.
- The program page says the Faculty of Engineering offers $2.5M+ in entrance scholarships and research awards for students entering Level 1 engineering programs.
- The supplementary applications page lists iBioMed as having a required supplementary application.

### McMaster scholarship snapshot
- McMaster's automatic entrance award table shows:
  - Computer Science at 97.00%+ gets $3,000
  - iBioMed at 98.00%+ gets $3,000
  - Faculty of Health Sciences at 98.00%+ gets $1,000
  - Nursing at 93.00%+ gets $1,000
  - Midwifery at 93.00%+ gets $1,000
- McMaster's faculty-specific award table also shows Honours Health Sciences at 93% to 97.99% gets $500.
