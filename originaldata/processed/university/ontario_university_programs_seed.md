# Ontario University Program Seed

This seed set captures a small group of high-frequency Ontario undergraduate programs and their official admission requirements from current university pages.

It is intentionally focused on questions students ask most often:
- what courses are required
- what average is competitive
- whether a supplemental application is required
- whether there are recommended or preferred courses

Tuition, scholarships, and full curriculum maps can be added in a second pass.

## University of Waterloo

### Computer Science
- Campus: Waterloo
- Ontario students need six Grade 12 U and/or M courses including Advanced Functions, Calculus and Vectors, any Grade 12 U English, and one other 4U course.
- Recommended: Grade 11 U Introduction to Computer Science.
- Admission average: individual selection from the low to mid-90s.
- Application note: apply directly; the Admission Information Form is required.

### Software Engineering
- Ontario students need six Grade 12 U and/or M courses including Advanced Functions, Calculus and Vectors, Chemistry, Physics, and English.
- The page also says applicants need programming experience, such as Grade 11 or 12 computing/information science or equivalent, or self-study with a demonstrated interest in solving open problems, debugging code, testing code, and learning new software languages.
- Admission average: individual selection from the low to mid-90s.
- Application note: the Admission Information Form is required and online interviews are required.

### Computing and Financial Management
- Ontario students need six Grade 12 U and/or M courses including any Grade 12 U English with a minimum final grade of 75%, Advanced Functions, Calculus and Vectors, and one other Grade 12 U course.
- Recommended: Introduction to Computer Science and Principles of Financial Accounting.
- Admission average: individual selection from the low to mid-90s.
- Application note: the Admission Information Form is required.
- Extra note: Waterloo strongly encourages the Canadian Senior Mathematics Contest and/or Euclid Mathematics Contest.

## University of Toronto

### Computer Science at U of T Mississauga
- OSSD with six subjects at the 4U/M level, including English.
- The co-op component of Grade 12 courses is not used for admission purposes.
- Ontario secondary school qualifications shown on the page: ENG4U / EAE4U, MHF4U, and MCV4U.

### Computer Science at U of T Scarborough
- OSSD with six subjects at the 4U/M level, including English.
- The co-op component of Grade 12 courses is not used for admission purposes.
- Ontario secondary school qualifications shown on the page: ENG4U / EAE4U, MCV4U, and MHF4U.

### Commerce
- OSSD with six subjects at the 4U/M level, including English.
- The co-op component of Grade 12 courses is not used for admission purposes.
- Ontario secondary school qualifications shown on the page: ENG4U / EAE4U, MCV4U, and MHF4U.

### Engineering Science
- OSSD with six subjects at the 4U/M level, including English.
- The co-op component of Grade 12 courses is not used for admission purposes.
- Ontario secondary school qualifications shown on the page: ENG4U / EAE4U, MHF4U, MCV4U, SCH4U, and SPH4U.

## Toronto Metropolitan University

### Business Management
- Ontario high school applicants need the OSSD, six Grade 12 U or M courses, and a minimum overall average of 70%.
- Required Grade 12 U courses: English and Mathematics, with ENG4U/EAE4U preferred and one of MCV4U, MHF4U, or MDM4U accepted.
- MCV4U is preferred.
- Due to competition, the average needed can fluctuate year to year.

### Computer Science
- Ontario high school applicants need the OSSD, six Grade 12 U or M courses, and a minimum overall average of 70%.
- Required Grade 12 U courses: ENG4U/EAE4U preferred, MHF4U, one of MCV4U or MDM4U, and one of SPH4U, SCH4U, or SBI4U.
- Due to competition, the average needed can fluctuate year to year.

## McMaster University

### Honours Health Sciences
- The program page says the Honours Health Sciences program requires a course that is not math, science, or technology-based for admission to Level I.
- The page also shows an anticipated admission average in the mid-80s.
- The supplementary applications page lists Honours Health Sciences as having a supplementary application due in the early/mid-February window.
